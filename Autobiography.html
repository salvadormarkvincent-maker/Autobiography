The Autobiography Of

Mark Vincent Salvador

A story of family, growth, and technology.
Chapter One

Who I Am

My name is Mark Vincent Salvador, and I am 19 years old. I live in Barangay Santiago, Iligan City. I spend most of my time studying and enjoying life with my family. Right now, I am a student at MSU-IIT, which is one of the best schools in the Philippines.

I am studying Computer Application, focusing on the Internet of Things (IoT). This course is about using technology to connect devices and make life easier. I am a simple person who loves family, learning, and growing as a person.

Chapter Two

The Quiet Years

When I was a baby, I lived with my Nanay because my parents were busy with their business. They visited me sometimes, but I mostly stayed at Nanay’s house.

Unlike other kids who played outside, I spent a lot of time indoors. My parents and Nanay didn't want me to play outside because they were worried I might learn bad habits. So, I grew up watching TV and playing with my toys instead. I learned to enjoy movies and stories inside the house.

Chapter Three

The Path of Learning

I started school at Berean Christian Academy from nursery to Grade 1. Then, I moved to Cabili Village Elementary School to finish elementary.

For high school and senior high, I studied at St. Michael’s College. It helped me get ready for college. Now, I am proud to be studying at MSU-IIT. My course is Bachelor of Science in Computer Application, major in Internet of Things.

Chapter Four

Beyond the Classroom

I don't just study all the time. I also have hobbies that I enjoy.

  • Playing Games

    I love playing online games. I like the challenge and competing with other players. It is also a way for me to have fun with my friends.

  • Reading Comics

    I also like reading Manhwa, which are Korean comics. They have interesting stories. Reading helps me relax after studying.

Chapter Five

Looking Forward

My biggest goal is to make my parents proud. They worked hard for me, and I want to succeed for them. I want to be someone my family can depend on.

In the future, I want to be a good father and a loving husband. I want to take care of my family and give them a good life, just like my parents did for me.

"Our greatest glory is not in never failing, but in rising up every time we fail."
– Ralph Waldo Emerson

This quote means that failure is not the end. Everyone makes mistakes, but we must stand up again. It teaches us to be strong even when things are hard. It reminds me to keep moving forward and never give up on my dreams.
